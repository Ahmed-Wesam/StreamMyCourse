# Stream My Course — What you get

**Your own online academy** where you add courses, students watch videos, take quizzes, and pay a **monthly membership** for access to your full library.

---

## For you (the teacher)

### Your branded website

- A **professional student website** for your academy—not a generic template students have to figure out
- **Your brand** on the pages students see: course covers, descriptions, and a polished course catalog
- A **course details page** for each offering so you can present what students get before they subscribe
- A **membership and pricing** area where students understand your plan and sign up
- **Preview anytime** from your teacher workspace so you see exactly what learners see

### Your control panel

- Sign in with **Google**
- See all your courses in one place
- **Create** a new course anytime
- **Save as draft** until you are ready, then **publish** when your course is ready to go live
- **Edit** course title and description
- **Delete** a course when you no longer need it
- **Upload a cover image** for each course
- Open the **student site** in one click to preview what learners see
- **Sign out** when you are done

### Organize your content

- Split each course into **sections**
- Add **lessons** inside each section
- **Upload video lessons** from your computer in the browser
- **Rename or remove** lessons when you need to update the course

### Quizzes

- Build **question banks** with multiple-choice questions
- Attach a quiz to a **section** of the course
- Choose how many questions students see each time
- Students receive a **score** and can **try again** with fresh questions

### Payments

- Offer a **monthly subscription** that unlocks **all your published courses**
- Students subscribe once and learn across your catalog
- **You** receive the payments as the merchant; the platform runs checkout and renewals for you
- A simple **payment setup** page guides you through getting ready to accept students

---

## For your students

- **Browse** your published courses on your branded site
- **View course details** and what is included
- **Sign in with Google** to start learning
- **My courses** — everything they can access in one place, with **progress at a glance**
- **Progress tracking** — see how far they are through each course, pick up the **next lesson** automatically, and **resume videos** right where they stopped
- **Watch videos** on phone, tablet, or computer
- **Take section quizzes** and see how they did
- **Subscribe** to get access and **manage their membership** from their account

## Why teachers choose Stream My Course

- **Your brand, your site** — students learn on a dedicated academy experience, not a crowded marketplace
- **Built for video teaching** — courses are organized around lessons students watch, not just documents
- **Progress built in** — learners always know what is next and can finish courses without losing their place
- **You stay in control** — create, organize, publish, and update your own content
- **Quizzes included** — check understanding without juggling a separate quiz tool
- **Membership model** — one monthly fee for students who want your full academy
- **Two clear experiences** — a focused learning site for students and a dedicated workspace for you
